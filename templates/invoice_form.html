Fields

FieldMeaning
invoice_numberFree text, shown as-is. Leave the pre-filled suggestion or type your own — nothing checks it for uniqueness.
from_name, from_address, from_phone, from_emailYour side of the letterhead. Any can be left blank.
to_name, to_address, to_phone, to_emailThe client's side.
invoice_date, due_dateYYYY-MM-DD. due_date shows "—" if blank.
payment_methodFree text, e.g. "Bank transfer" or "Check".
theme_colorHex color (e.g. #2955a3) for the accent bar/table header — also settable via the color picker on the Create tab.
account_detailsFree text (bank/account/routing, PayPal handle, etc). Only printed if non-empty.
notesFree text, printed at the bottom if non-empty.
itemsList of {"description": "...", "amount": 0}. The total is always the sum of these.
